Pill border derby

Same pill, same label, five border treatments. Shown on Paper light and dark with a card nearby so you can judge how loud it is next to the app.

1 · Spectrum ring

Full RGB conic gradient spinning at 3s with a soft matching glow behind. The classic "RGB" look. Loudest of the set; reads gamer-hardware if the speed is wrong.

2 · Aurora

Brand-adjacent hues (berry, amber, lavender, grape, sky) on a thin 1.5px rim with a wide blurred halo that breathes. Slow 7s drift. Premium rather than gamer; the glow does the talking.

3 · Comet

One bright berry-to-white arc orbits the edge with an eased speed (faster on the long sides, slower on the ends), leaving a quiet border the rest of the way. Attention without a rainbow.

4 · Dual chase

Two arcs, grape and berry, chase each other around a near-black pill. Strictly the brand palette, so it ties to RainFocus while still clearly being "the special button."

5 · Hue shift

The whole pill slowly cycles through the spectrum (8s per revolution) with a lighter rim of the same hue spinning on top and a shadow that follows the color. The most literally "RGB." Mesmerizing or distracting depending on taste.
