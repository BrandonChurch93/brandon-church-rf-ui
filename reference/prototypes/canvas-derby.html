C · Lavender

The canvas carries a faint grape tint so the whole app feels branded, with lavender lines instead of gray. Dark mode is a deep violet-black where glows and light sweeps look their best. The most "RainFocus" of the three and the most cinematic in dark; the risk is that purple-on-purple gets heavy if the Builder isn't disciplined about where accent goes.
